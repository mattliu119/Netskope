Netskope One · Netskope Private Access (NPA) · ZTNA

ZTNA 取代 VPN：連到應用，而不是連進網路 ZTNA replaces VPN: connect to apps, not to the network

VPN 一連上就等於進到內網，使用者與被入侵的裝置都能看到整個網段。Netskope Private Access 依身分與裝置狀態，逐一授權個別應用；沒被授權的主機根本看不到，連線內容還能用 TP 與 DLP 檢查。資料中心的 Publisher 只往外連線，不必在防火牆開放任何對外服務埠。 Once a VPN connects, the user is on the internal network, and so is any compromised device. Netskope Private Access authorizes individual apps by identity and device posture; unauthorized hosts are simply invisible, and what flows through can be inspected with TP and DLP. The Publisher in the data center only connects outbound, so no inbound ports open on the firewall.
