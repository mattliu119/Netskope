AI Command Center：一張圖看清公司裡所有的 AI AI Command Center: every AI in the company on one map

不需要額外部署，直接用 Netskope 已經看到的流量，自動盤點 AI 應用、MCP Server、Agent 與模型（包含沒人申報的 Shadow AI）。每個資產都附上誰在用、從哪台裝置、資料流向哪裡，風險對應到合規框架並給出修正步驟；AISecOps 再把上百筆事件自動調查成一份結論，告訴你下一步該做什麼。 No extra deployment: using the traffic Netskope already sees, it inventories AI apps, MCP servers, agents and models, including unreported shadow AI. Each asset shows who uses it, from which device and where data goes; risks map to compliance frameworks with fix steps, and AISecOps automatically investigates hundreds of events into one verdict that says what to do next.
