GenAI 管控：只放行企業帳號，個人版一律阻擋 GenAI Control: Corporate Accounts Only, Personal Blocked

瀏覽器與桌面 App 的 GenAI 流量都由 Netskope Client 導入 NewEdge。CASB 的 Instance Awareness 分辨企業版與個人版：個人版直接阻擋，企業版再經 NG SWG、CASB Inline、DLP 與 AI Guardrails 逐關檢查。 GenAI traffic from browsers and desktop apps is steered to NewEdge by the Netskope Client. CASB instance awareness tells corporate from personal accounts: personal is blocked outright, and corporate traffic is checked by NG SWG, CASB inline, DLP and AI Guardrails in turn.

02 · Control Points

四道控制點各管什麼What each control point does

瀏覽器與桌面 App 一樣受控Browsers and desktop apps are controlled the same way

Netskope Client 在端點導流，不論使用者從瀏覽器開 ChatGPT、Claude，或使用桌面版 App，流量都會進入同一組控制點、套用同一套政策。部分桌面 App 若使用憑證綁定（certificate pinning）等機制，可能需要額外的例外或設定，建議 PoC 時逐一驗證要納管的 App。The Netskope Client steers traffic on the endpoint, so whether users open ChatGPT or Claude in a browser or a desktop app, traffic passes the same control points under the same policy. Some desktop apps that use certificate pinning may need exceptions or extra setup; verify each app in scope during the PoC.
