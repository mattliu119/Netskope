多據點 SASE 現代化轉型 Multi-Site SASE Modernization

以 Netskope BWAN 實現極簡網路與全面防護 Simpler networking and full protection with Netskope BWAN

把各分支防火牆與 HQ 防火牆統一置換為 Netskope Borderless WAN Gateway。Internet 與 SaaS 流量就近送往 Netskope 雲端做資安檢查，分點到 HQ 內部系統走 BWAN 加密 Overlay，不再回繞總部、也不必逐點維護 VPN。 Replace every branch firewall and the HQ firewall with Netskope Borderless WAN gateways. Internet and SaaS traffic goes to the nearest Netskope cloud POP for inspection, and branch-to-HQ traffic rides the encrypted BWAN overlay, with no hairpinning through HQ and no per-site VPNs to maintain.

03 · Proposal Highlights

提案四大亮點Four Proposal Highlights

架構簡化、降低 TCOSimpler architecture, lower TCO

不再為每個分點採購、升級與維保獨立防火牆。BWAN 以零接觸部署（ZTP）上線，全點使用標準化設定，由雲端統一交付。No more buying, upgrading and maintaining a firewall per site. BWAN comes online with zero-touch provisioning (ZTP) and every site gets a standard config delivered from the cloud.

HQ Hub 高可用HQ hub high availability

HQ 建議規劃雙 BWAN（主備或雙活）搭配雙 ISP 線路，確保數十到上百個分點存取內部系統不中斷。Plan dual BWAN gateways at HQ (active/standby or active/active) on two ISPs so dozens to hundreds of sites keep reaching internal systems.

多線路彈性（固網 + 4G / 5G）Multi-WAN (fixed + 4G / 5G)

分點拉固網不便或需要備援時，可採固網加 4G / 5G SIM 混合線路，主線路異常自動切換。實際切換時間依線路與設定而定，建議於 PoC 實測。Where fixed lines are hard to get or backup is needed, mix fixed broadband with 4G / 5G SIMs and fail over automatically. Actual failover time depends on links and config; measure it in the PoC.

端到端零信任延伸Zero trust beyond the site

現階段由辦公室設備經 BWAN 上雲；未來外勤人員的手機或筆電安裝 Netskope Client，即可套用同一套資安政策並存取 HQ 系統。Today office devices reach the cloud through BWAN; later, field staff install the Netskope Client on phones or laptops and get the same policies and HQ access.
